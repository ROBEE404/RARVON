# Privacy

The full policy is the in-app Privacy page (`/privacy`, source: `src/pages/Privacy.tsx`). In short:

- **Files never leave the device.** Every tool works inside the browser; Rarvon has no server that receives files.
- **No account, no ads, no cookies, no analytics, no trackers**, now or later.
- **Saved on the device only:** theme, language, recently used tools, Image to Text settings, Video Editor shortcuts
  and projects (including copies of added videos), downloaded AI models and offline files. All can be removed by
  clearing the site's data; Video Editor projects can also be deleted in the editor.
- **Downloads come only from Rarvon's own site** (AI models, OCR language files, the MP3 encoder). Nothing is loaded
  from other companies' servers.
- **The website host** sees ordinary visit logs (IP address, browser, page, time), like any website. They never
  contain files.

If you change what Rarvon stores or loads, update the Privacy page (and its date) in the same change.
